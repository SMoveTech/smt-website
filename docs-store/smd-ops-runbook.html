S-Move Dealer — Operations & Incident Runbook

Known failure modes and their fixes, plus what monitoring exists today.

Compiled 2026-07-30 from the SMD App codebase and prior incident history.

1. Where to look first

  • Railway deploy/runtime logs — the first stop for any crash or 500. A crash-on-boot almost always means MODULE_NOT_FOUND (see §2) or a missing/misnamed env var.
  • Admin SMS alerts — sms.js texts the numbers in ADMIN_ALERT_NUMBERS on business applications and other key events. This runs over SMO's Twilio number (SMD has no phone number on its own Twilio account), so an alert failure could mean either Twilio account is misconfigured.
  • Stripe dashboard — webhook delivery logs show every attempt and response code; check here first for anything payment/subscription related before assuming the app is broken.

There is currently no uptime monitoring or error-tracking service (no Sentry/equivalent found in dependencies) — the only signals are Railway's own logs, the admin SMS alerts, and users reporting problems. Worth treating as a gap.

3. Automatic self-healing (not incidents — expected behaviour)

  • Stale checkout reservations expire automatically every 60 seconds.
  • Past-due sponsorships are dropped automatically by the dunning sweep (every 6 hours) after a configurable grace period — don't manually intervene unless a seller disputes it.
  • Soft-deleted financial records are purged after a 14-day grace window; unconverted canvass-target product photos purge after 30 days. Neither needs manual cleanup.

4. What's NOT covered here yet

  • No documented incident-severity classification or on-call rotation — currently informal (whoever notices first).
  • No uptime/error monitoring service — see §1. Worth adding before the marketplace opens more widely.
  • Restoring from a backup after data loss is covered separately in the Backup & Disaster Recovery doc — and currently has no dedicated restore tool, only the raw backup files.
