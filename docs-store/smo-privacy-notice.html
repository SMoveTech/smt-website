Who we are

S-Move Removals Ltd ("S-Move", "we") is the data controller for the personal information you give us when you request a quote or book a move. We are registered in Scotland (company number SC892140). If you have any questions about your data, contact us at alex@s-move.co.uk [confirm the address you want customers to use].

What information we collect

  • Your details: name, email, phone number.
  • Your move: collection and delivery addresses, move date, property details, an inventory of items, and any notes or photos you share with us.
  • Payments: the amount you pay and payment status. Card payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.
  • Communications: messages, emails, texts and calls between you and us (our phone and chat use an AI assistant to help handle enquiries).

How and why we use it

WhyOur lawful basis
To quote for and carry out your move, and to keep you updatedPerformance of our contract with you
To take payment and keep accounting recordsLegal obligation (tax/accounting) and contract
To keep your account and our systems secureOur legitimate interest in security
To ask for a review, or send you marketing (only if you've agreed)Consent — you can withdraw it any time

Our use of AI

We use an AI assistant (provided by Anthropic) to help handle enquiries by chat, phone and text, to read room photos you upload, and to help prepare quotes. Our staff make the final decisions — the AI does not decide your price. We don't send special-category data (such as health information) to the AI, and under Anthropic's terms your information is not used to train their models.

Who we share it with

We only share your data with the service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — secure database and file hosting.
  • Stripe — card payments and direct debit.
  • Twilio — phone and SMS.
  • Google (Gmail / Maps) — email and address look-up.
  • Anthropic — the AI assistant described above.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK; where that's the case, your data is protected by appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses / the UK IDTA). We do not sell your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your job and communication records for up to 6 years after your move (to cover our contract and legal obligations), and financial records for at least 6 years as required by law. Marketing preferences are kept until you withdraw them. After the retention period we delete or anonymise your data.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it (where we're not legally required to keep it); provide it in a portable format; or stop using it for certain purposes (such as marketing). To exercise any of these, email alex@s-move.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Keeping your data safe

Your information travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, passwords are stored in a one-way encrypted form, card details are handled entirely by Stripe, and access to your records is tightly restricted. Your personal portal link is private to you — please don't share it with anyone you don't trust.
