S-Move — Customer Portal User Guide

Your personal portal for managing your move from start to finish — get a quote, accept it, pay, and stay in touch, all in one place, 24/7 from any device.

Rebuilt 2026-07-29 for S-Move (replaces the old "RemovalDesk" white-label guide).

1. Welcome to your customer portal

Your customer portal is your personal, secure space to manage everything about your removal. What you can do in it:

  • Get an accurate removal quote through our smart assistant
  • View, accept or give feedback on your quote
  • Pay your deposit and balance securely by card or bank transfer
  • Request date changes, packing materials or additional services
  • View all your documents (quote, invoice, T&Cs)
  • Message the S-Move team directly
  • Update your contact details and add location contacts
  • Request additional jobs at any time

2. Getting started — getting a quote

What you'll need before you begin:

  • Your collection address (where we're picking up from)
  • Your delivery address (where we're taking things to)
  • Your preferred move date (approximate is fine)
  • Your email address and a password you'll remember

Registration steps:

  1. Visit the portal link provided by S-Move (or click "Get a free quote" on the website).
  2. Select your job type — House Move, House Clearance, Waste Disposal, etc.
  3. Enter your personal details: name, email, phone and a password.
  4. Enter your collection and delivery addresses and preferred move date.
  5. Add any notes that might be useful (access issues, special items, etc.).
  6. Click Submit & request quote.
  7. You'll be taken straight to the Messages tab, where our assistant starts gathering details.

Tip: Bookmark your portal link — your personal link gives you instant access without logging in again on the same device.

3. The quote assistant

After registering you'll be connected to our automated quote assistant. It asks questions to build an accurate inventory of your items, so we can send the right size vehicle and crew. It goes room by room, and only needs furniture and bulky items — not clothing, books or kitchen bits (those are covered by the box count).

What it will ask about:

  • Furniture in each room (sofas, beds, wardrobes, tables, etc.)
  • Approximate number of boxes/bags you'll pack yourself
  • Any special or heavy items (piano, gym equipment, safe, mounted TV)
  • Loft or attic contents
  • Access at each address (floor, lift availability, parking)
  • Whether you'd like our professional packing service
  • Whether you need packing materials (boxes, bubble wrap, tape)
  • Your preferred move type (key exchange, keys prior, or storage needed)
  • How you heard about us

Note: The assistant never asks you to estimate van size or volume — just tell it what you have and it handles the rest.

Tip: If a room is tricky to describe, upload photos via the Documents tab and our team will factor them into your quote.

4. Viewing & accepting your quote

Once our team has reviewed your inventory you'll get a personalised quote — you'll see a notification in your portal and may get an email too.

To view it: open your portal → Documents tab → View Quote PDF for the full breakdown.

To accept it:

  1. A banner appears on your home screen when a quote is ready to accept.
  2. Click View quote & respond.
  3. Read the Terms & Conditions and tick the checkbox to confirm.
  4. Click Accept quote.
  5. You'll be prompted to pay your deposit to lock in your move date.

Want changes? Use the Messages tab to chat with the team — they can issue a revised quote at any time.

5. Making payments

Deposit: a 50% deposit confirms your booking and locks in your date. You're prompted to pay it right after accepting your quote.

Payment methods:

  • Card — pay instantly by debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard).
  • Bank transfer — transfer directly to our account (details shown in the portal).

Balance: the remaining balance is due 3 days before your move date. You'll get a portal + email reminder 5 days before.

Note: All payments are processed securely. Your card details are never stored on our systems.

6. Requesting changes & extra services

You can request changes any time through the Requests tab — even after accepting your quote.

  • Date change — go to Change Date, enter your preferred new date, an alternative, and the reason. The team responds within 24 hours.
  • Packing materials — Requests → Packing materials; enter quantities (prices shown per item) and submit.
  • Packing service — Requests → Packing service; choose full packing, fragiles only, or unpacking at destination.
  • Storage — Requests → Storage; describe what you need storing and for how long, and we'll come back with availability and pricing.

7. Other portal tabs

  • Documents — download your quote PDF and invoice PDF, view the T&Cs, and upload room/item photos to help with your quote.
  • Messages — chat directly with the S-Move team during business hours. A badge shows when you have an unread reply.
  • Your Details (on the My Jobs tab) — click Edit to update your name, phone or email, and add contacts who'll be present at each address on moving day.

8. Requesting a new job

Already have an account? Request another job any time without re-registering:

  1. Open your portal and scroll to My Bookings.
  2. Click + Request a new job.
  3. Select the job type and enter the addresses and date.
  4. Add any useful notes and click Request quote.
  5. You'll be taken to Messages to continue with the quote assistant.

9. FAQ

Q: I've forgotten my password — how do I get back in?
On the Sign In screen, click "Forgot password? Send my portal link by email" and enter your email. If it's registered, you'll get a link within a few minutes; once in, update your password from My Details.

Q: I've lost my portal link — how do I access my account?
Go to the portal website, click Sign In (top right), and enter your email and password to log in from any device.

Q: Can I change my mind after accepting?
Yes — use Messages to speak with the team. Changes to scope may mean a revised quote. Cancellations are subject to the T&Cs you accepted.

Q: Will my data be safe?
Your portal is protected by your personal password. Never share your portal link with anyone you don't trust — it gives direct access to your booking.

Q: What if I don't hear back from the assistant?
It usually replies within a minute. If you see a "feel free to send us a message" prompt, just type a message and the team will pick it up. You can also call us on 07414 140 899.
