S-Move — Staff Portal User Guide

For drivers and porters: everything you need to manage your shifts, submit your hours and keep your details up to date. Works on any device — phone, tablet or laptop.

Rebuilt 2026-07-29 for S-Move (replaces the old "RemovalDesk" white-label guide).

1. Logging in

  1. Go to the portal link provided by your manager (or the /staff URL).
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Tap Sign in.
  4. You'll land on the My Shifts tab.

Note: Forgotten your password? Contact your manager to have it reset. You can also set up fingerprint / Face ID sign-in from My Details once you're logged in (see §7), so you don't have to type your password each time on your own device.

2. My Shifts

The My Shifts tab is your home screen. It shows your confirmed shifts, past and upcoming.

  • Upcoming shifts — a simple card with the date and your role (Driver or Porter). Full job details only appear on the day of the shift.
  • Today's shifts — on the day, you see full details: customer name and contact number; collection and delivery addresses (with tap-to-navigate links); job type; start time and meeting location; admin notes from the office; and, for flex tasks, what materials to deliver/collect and any notes.
  • Past shifts — your history. If a finish time hasn't been approved yet, it shows as "awaiting approval".

3. Available shifts

The Available tab shows open shifts you can apply for. Only shifts matching your role (driver/porter) appear.

  1. Tap Available.
  2. Browse the shifts — each shows the date, role and job reference.
  3. Tap Apply on one you'd like to work.
  4. The manager confirms or declines your application.
  5. Once confirmed, it appears in your My Shifts tab.

Note: Once a shift is filled by someone else it disappears from this list automatically.

4. Requesting to be removed from a shift

If you need to come off a confirmed shift, use the Cancel Shift tab to send a request to your manager. You stay on the shift until it's confirmed.

  1. Tap Cancel Shift.
  2. Select the shift.
  3. Choose a reason and add any notes.
  4. Tap Submit request.
  5. Wait for confirmation — do not assume you're off until it's confirmed.

5. Calendar

The Calendar tab gives you a monthly view. Days with confirmed shifts are highlighted, and days with open available shifts are marked too. Tap any date to see that day's shifts.

Tip: The calendar scrolls to today automatically when you open it.

6. On the job — completing & submitting your time

Marking a job complete (tells the office the job is finished):

  1. Find today's job on My Shifts.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the shift card.
  3. Tap Mark job as complete (for material delivery/collection, tap Mark task as complete instead).

Submitting your finish time (so your hours get logged for payroll):

  1. On the shift card, scroll to Log finish time.
  2. Enter the time you finished (e.g. 14:30).
  3. Tap Submit finish time.
  4. Your manager reviews and approves it.
  5. Once approved, the time is locked and appears on your payslip.

7. Flex tasks (material deliveries & collections)

Sometimes you'll get a flex task — a smaller job like delivering packing materials before a move or collecting them afterwards. On the shift card you'll see a green banner with the task name, the customer's address with a navigate link, the list of materials, and any office notes (e.g. "customer out — leave in porch"). Tap Mark task as complete at the bottom when done.

8. My Details

Keep your info up to date in the My Details tab. Changes are sent to your manager for approval before they take effect. You can update:

  • Contact details (phone, email)
  • Emergency contact name, phone and relationship
  • Bank account details for payroll (sort code and account number)
  • Password
  • Fingerprint / Face ID sign-in — register this device so you can sign in with biometrics instead of your password.

Note: Your manager must approve any change to bank details before it's applied.

9. Payslips

Payslips are generated weekly once your finish times are approved. Open the Payslips tab to view them.

  • Each payslip shows the week's dates, shifts worked, hours, rate and total pay.
  • A notification appears when a new payslip is available.
  • Payslips can't be edited once approved — if there's an error, contact your manager.
