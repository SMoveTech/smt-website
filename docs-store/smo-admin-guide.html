S-Move Operations — Admin Console User Guide

The complete guide to running the removals business from one screen: customers, quotes, staff, requests, payments and full financials.

Rebuilt 2026-07-29 for S-Move Removals Ltd's live instance (replaces the old "RemovalDesk" white-label guide). Reflects the console as it works today.

1. Logging in & access

  • The Admin Console lives at app.s-move.co.uk/admin. Sign in with your own named account (email + password) — there is no shared login.
  • Admin access is device-gated as a second factor: a machine has to be approved before it will even show the login form. On an unapproved device you'll see a "this machine isn't approved" page (HTTP 403) rather than the password box — that is expected, not a fault. A new device is approved via an invite/setup link from someone already in.
  • If you think you've "forgotten your password" but you're actually being stopped before the password screen, that's the device gate — get your device approved rather than resetting the password.
  • Keep your password somewhere safe. Financial and customer data is never hard-deleted, but losing the only admin login still means going through device re-approval to get back in.

3. Customer list

The Customers tab shows all active jobs. Click any row to open the full job card.

  • Job Ref — unique job number (e.g. #0001)
  • Customer — name, email, phone, job type badge
  • Date — move date (click to jump to the Diary)
  • Quote — current status and amount
  • Booking — stage (Enquiry, Quoted, Confirmed, etc.)
  • Payment — Unpaid, Deposit paid, Paid in full

The stats bar at the top updates live — Total Customers, Pending Quotes, Confirmed Moves, Payments Due. Use Search to find by name, email, phone or job ref, and the quote-status pills to filter. New Job (top right) creates a job manually.

4. Job cards

Click any customer to open their job card. Key sub-tabs inside:

  • Quote Breakdown — enter costs, send quotes, issue revisions. A £50 minimum warning prevents accidental zero quotes. You can enter a quote inclusive of VAT and it will break the net/VAT out for you.
  • Payments — confirm bank transfers, approve payment-link requests.
  • Date changes — approve or decline customer date-change requests.
  • Service requests — packing materials and services with auto-calculated costs.
  • Messages — full chat thread; reply to the customer directly from here.
  • Photos — room/item photos the customer uploaded for the quote.
  • Communications — the log of emails and SMS sent for this job, with sent dates.
  • Rota Info — set crew size and confirm the job for the rota.

5. Sending quotes, invoices & emails

Customer emails are never auto-sent on a status change — you always send them deliberately from a Send panel, so nothing goes out by accident.

  1. Open the job card → Quote Breakdown.
  2. Enter the removal service cost (other fields optional). The total auto-calculates, or enter it manually.
  3. Save — the quote status sets to "Sent".
  4. To email the customer, open the Send panel: you get a PDF preview of the exact document first, then send. The paired customer SMS goes with it, and you get a Gmail "landed in Sent" confirmation so you know it actually left.
  5. Each document tracks its own sent date, so you can see at a glance what has and hasn't gone out.

Invoices work the same way (preview → send). For company-account jobs the email opt-out is taken from the account, so a stale per-job opt-out won't silently block a send.

AI quote suggestion: when pricing a job the console can suggest a price and explain its reasoning, learning from past quotes. It's a suggestion — you stay in control of the final number.

6. Staff Rota

  1. Open the job card → Rota Info → enter driver/porter count → Confirm.
  2. Go to the Staff Rota tab → click the job date.
  3. Drag the job into Team A, B or C.
  4. Use the dropdowns to assign specific staff members.
  5. Click Post Shifts — staff can now apply via their portal.

Flexible Jobs panel (right of the Rota): material delivery/collection tasks and no-fixed-date jobs. Assign them to a crew (A/B/C) on a chosen day. A badge on the tab flags shift-withdrawal requests waiting on you.

7. HR & staff management

Adding a staff member:

  1. HR tab → + Add Staff Member.
  2. Enter name, email, phone and role (Driver / Porter / Class 2).
  3. Save, then open their HR card to set a password.
  4. Click Send Portal Invite + User Guide to email them their login and the staff user guide automatically.

Staff are grouped by role: Admin → Class 2 Driver → Driver → Porter → Casual Staff. The HR tab is also where payroll and staff-change approvals live. Payroll hours come from the recorded start time of the shift, not the job's arrival time.

8. Financials

The Financials tab is the money hub. It carries:

  • Income & Expenditure (I&E) — the ledger, with gross / net / VAT columns and job-ref linking. Card income can be batch-reconciled against Stripe.
  • VAT — VAT has been live since 1 Jul 2026 at the standard 20% rate; the ledger tracks it per line.
  • VAT Pot — shows net VAT due against the live Monzo VAT pot balance, plus a buffer, so you can see whether enough is set aside.
  • Petty Cash — log a staff out-of-pocket expense with its receipt. It posts to I&E as expenditure immediately (category "Staff Reimbursement") so the accountant sees it, and it's reimbursed on the staff member's next payslip as a separate non-taxable line (wages + expenses = total paid). Admin-only.

Bank activity auto-syncs from Monzo (friendly categories, notes kept, pot transfers filtered). Receipts uploaded against a transaction have their VAT read and applied automatically. Income can be matched to jobs by reference + name to set payment status. Financial data is never hard-deleted — corrections are made in place.

9. Pricing, Requests & company Accounts

  • Pricing — set unit prices for packing materials (small box, large box, tape, bubble wrap). These show to customers in real time when they request materials. Enter amounts → Save Prices.
  • Requests — everything awaiting your approval, with a count badge. Packing material requests auto-calculate the cost (adjust for a discount, then Approve → adds to the invoice and notifies the customer). Date-change requests — review the preferred/alternative dates → Approve or Decline. Check this every morning.
  • Accounts — company/business accounts. Their jobs can use standard company job types and the invoices carry the company logo and billing address.

10. Ask S-Move (in-console co-pilot)

"Ask S-Move" is a built-in AI helper that can explain how things work and point you to the right place in the console. Text size is adjustable per user from the display settings.

11. Daily-use tips

  • Check the Requests tab every morning — packing approvals update the customer invoice automatically.
  • Dates on job cards are clickable — tap a move date to jump to that day in the Diary.
  • When a quote is accepted, go to Rota Info immediately to plan the crew.
  • Use the Archive tab for completed/cancelled jobs — they stay in the records but leave the main list.
  • Always preview a document before sending it — the PDF preview shows the customer exactly what they'll get.
