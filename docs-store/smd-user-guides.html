S-Move Dealer — Seller & Shopper Guide

How to use S-Move Dealer as a seller running a shop, or as a shopper browsing and buying from local independents. Includes a Support/FAQ section at the end.

Compiled 2026-08-04 from a direct read of the live seller portal, item page and shopper home. Reflects the app as it works today.

1. Seller Guide

1.1 Becoming a seller

Applying is free and takes about two minutes at /seller/apply — Alex approves every seller personally, there's no automated gatekeeping beyond the basics (accepted terms, verified email + phone). Four steps to go live:

  1. Apply — fill in the short application form.
  2. Take photos — upload photos of your items; the built-in AI writes the title and description and suggests a price for you.
  3. We review — your listing goes live after a quick manual check.
  4. Buyers find you — customers browse your shop, enquire, and arrange collection directly with you. You keep 100% of every sale — S-Move Dealer never takes a cut.

No website needed — S-Move Dealer gives every shop its own branded page. If you were signed up via a canvasser visiting your shop, any photos and notes they captured carry straight over onto your new live shop.

1.3 Adding a listing

Upload photos on the New Listing screen; the AI suggests a title, description, category and price based on the photo and any staff notes you add — you can edit anything before it goes live. New/edited listings are re-checked before appearing on the marketplace. Listings can only be edited once every 24 hours, so review carefully before saving a change.

1.4 My Shop — personalising your page

The Profile section has four tabs:

  • Appearance — choose a colour preset or fine-tune every colour and background pattern of your public shop page, with a live preview. This is also where "Pause my shop" (holiday mode) lives: it hides your listings behind a friendly custom message without withdrawing anything, and you can switch it back on any time.
  • Listings — choose how your items are ordered on your shop page (newest first, most popular, price low/high, or grouped by category), and pin specific items as "Featured" at the very top regardless of that order.
  • Delivery — if you handle delivery of large items yourself, switch this on and S-Move Removals delivery quotes won't be offered on your large/heavy items; your own delivery details (set under Details) show instead.
  • Details — logo upload (trade sellers), and your account/payment/delivery information shown to shoppers.

1.5 Enquiries — how a sale actually happens

No money moves through S-Move Dealer for an item purchase. A buyer sends an enquiry (collection, delivery, or a general question) straight to your Enquiries inbox; from there, you arrange collection/delivery and take payment directly with the buyer — however you'd normally do that. The item page also shows your preferred contact details to the shopper, so some buyers may contact you directly instead of enquiring through the form.

1.6 Testimonials

When a shopper gives you their free monthly support token (§2.5), they can leave a short note on why — these appear on your shop page. If one isn't accurate or appropriate, report it from the Testimonials tab and the team will review it.

1.7 Sponsor — the front-page sidebar

An optional flat monthly subscription puts your shop in the "Local Sponsors" panel on the S-Move Dealer front page — but where you sit within that panel is earned by real customers (their visits and votes), never bought. There's no bidding for the top spot, so a small shop can out-rank a big spender on merit alone. Cancel any time from the Sponsor tab.

2. Shopper Guide

2.1 Browsing

You can browse the whole directory and every shop's listings at shop.s-move.co.uk without creating an account — search by keyword, filter by category, or open a specific shop's page.

2.2 Creating an account

An account is only needed to enquire, save items, or support sellers with hearts/votes/donations. There's no password to remember: verify your email (and optionally your phone) with a one-time code, and you're signed in from then on.

2.3 Enquiring about an item

On any item page, choose Arrange Collection, Request S-Move Delivery (a delivery quote from S-Move Removals for large items, where offered), or a General Enquiry. Your message goes straight to the seller's Enquiries inbox. Every item page also has a built-in AI assistant that can answer questions about that specific item — condition, dimensions, collection, delivery — before you commit to an enquiry.

Payment for the item itself happens directly with the seller, arranged however you agree between you — S-Move Dealer isn't part of that transaction.

2.4 Wishlist — "notify me"

Can't find what you're after? Describe it (style, size, rough budget) via the chat assistant and it's added to a wishlist; you'll get an email as soon as something matching is listed.

2.5 Hearts & your monthly support token

Signed-in shoppers get one support token a month — your biggest way to help a favourite seller's ranking — plus unlimited cosmetic hearts any time. Neither can be bought, and neither is required to enquire or buy — they're purely a way to support the shops you like.

2.6 Supporting S-Move Dealer itself

Separately from supporting individual sellers, you can give a one-off or monthly gift to S-Move Dealer itself (the /donate page) to help keep the platform free for everyone. A gift here buys nothing and never affects any seller's ranking.

3. Support / FAQ

QuestionAnswer
Where's my order / how do I track delivery?S-Move Dealer doesn't process the sale itself — once you've enquired, collection/delivery and payment are arranged directly with the seller. Contact the seller through the enquiry thread for updates.
How do I pay for an item?Directly with the seller, by whatever method you agree — not through the S-Move Dealer app.
Is it free to sell on S-Move Dealer?Yes, completely free, forever — no commission on sales. The optional Sponsor subscription (§1.7) only buys a sidebar slot, never ranking.
I sent an enquiry and haven't heard back — what do I do?Enquiries go straight to the seller, not S-Move Dealer — try the shop's listed contact details on the item/shop page, or send a follow-up enquiry.
How do I edit or remove a listing?Edit from My Listings (§1.3) — allowed once every 24 hours, and re-checked before it goes live again. To remove a listing entirely, email alex@s-move.co.uk.
How do I delete my account or my data?Email alex@s-move.co.uk — see the Data Protection doc for how requests are handled.
Who do I contact for help?alex@s-move.co.uk for anything not covered above.
