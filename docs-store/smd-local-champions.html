SMD — Local Champions & Allies (people who could publicly support us)

Compiled 27 July 2026. Every name is from a public source. Roles change and some are mid-dispute or political — see handling notes. Never invent a quote; confirm current role before approaching by name.

Goal: find people already campaigning for local/independent high streets who'd lend a voice, an intro to shops, or coverage. Ranked warmest-first. SMD = free community directory + marketplace, free for shops, never charges to rank, high-street-revival mission.

⭐ Top 6 to approach first (best warmth × leverage)

  1. Scotland's Towns Partnership — Kimberley Guthrie (Chief Officer) (national, warmest partner)
    Runs Scotland Loves Local — the national shop-local campaign + gift card (physical-premises only, so SMD's online directory complements it). Comes with a ready-made distribution channel (Love Local Week, partner editorial). Frame as "we deliver Love Local on the ground in the Lothians," never a rival. Route: lovelocal.scot/contact-us · @scotlandstowns · 45 King Street, Stirling. (Address to Guthrie — NOT former chief Phil Prentice, who stepped back.)
  2. Philip Mellor — Chair, Dunbar Trades' Association (East Lothian, mission-identical)
    DTA already runs offline "Shop Local – Win Local" + "Spot the Malarky" campaigns with Visit East Lothian. SMD is the online version of what he already does. Route: dunbar.org.uk/dta · facebook.com/DunbarTradesAssociation.
  3. Emma Bouglet — Business Manager, East Lothian Food & Drink (BID) (East Lothian, multiplier)
    Scotland's first food-&-drink BID; her public words are textbook SMD ("every purchase with an independent… strengthens the fabric of our communities"). Reaches many member businesses at once. Route: scotlandsfooddrinkcounty.com.
  4. Mark Darragh — Deputy Chair, One Linlithgow BID (West Lothian, near-verbatim pitch match)
    Owner of Play Bugs; launched Scotland Loves Local Week for Linlithgow — "Every time someone chooses local, they're making an investment in our community." Warmest single indie voice found. Route: onelinlithgow.com. (Operational partner: Tony I'Anson, BID Manager.)
  5. Tom Donaldson — Chair, Dalkeith Means Business (Midlothian, pilot partner)
    Business-led group actively building a town directory + testing a BID, backed by Scotland's Towns Partnership. SMD directly serves what he's trying to build. Route: dalkeithmeansbusiness.co.uk. (Comms/route-in: Jill Simon.)
  6. Daniel Johnson MSP (Lab, Edinburgh Southern) (political — the standout)
    A Lothian MSP who was himself an independent retailer (ex-MD of Paper Tiger/Studio One, Morningside Traders member); convened the Economy & Fair Work Committee's town-centres inquiry. The most credible political voice for SMD. Keep cross-party — pair with Fiona Hyslop MSP (SNP, Linlithgow) for West Lothian so no endorsement reads as partisan. Route: constituency office / parliament.scot.

East Lothian (home turf — full list)

Tier 1 — warmest:

  • Philip Mellor — Dunbar Trades' Association (see above).
  • Emma Bouglet — East Lothian Food & Drink BID (see above).
  • Susan Oliver — Co-chair, North Berwick Business Association; owner, So&Co Accessories. Most media-active indie voice in ELdom. ⚠ Currently mid-dispute with the council over parking charges — engage ONLY on the positive "help shops be found & trade" angle; stay out of the parking row.

Tier 2 — community organisers (would amplify):

  • Mike Falconer / Neil Ellis — Tranent & Elphinstone Community Council; lobbying to fill empty units & "bring businesses back to Tranent High Street."
  • DJ Johnston-Smith — Chair, Prestonpans Community Council; leading town-centre regeneration advocacy.
  • Musselburgh Business Partnership — collective voice for the biggest town; named lead unconfirmed — approach via facebook.com/MusselburghBP to identify.

Tier 3 — sympathetic shop owners (founding listers / testimonials, not campaigners):

  • Chris Lockett (Lockett Bros, North Berwick), Meg Maitland (Maitland clothing, North Berwick), Blueberry (gift shop, Musselburgh — verify owner).

Media (coverage, not endorsement):

  • East Lothian Courier newsroom — editor Robbie Scott; reporters Sam Berkeley, Cameron Ritchie, Gregor Millar. The best local-launch coverage route. eastlothiancourier.com/contact · @elcourier.

🎯 Genuine opening: Haddington has NO active business association — Cllr George McGuire publicly called for one. SMD can be pitched as the ready-made focal point a Haddington group would otherwise have to build.

Edinburgh

  • Fawns Reid — Chair, Greater Grassmarket traders / BID; owner of Fabhatrix. Believer in banding indies for shared marketing. Warm. Route: greatergrassmarket.co.uk.
  • Morningside Traders Association — mission is literally "supporting local business partners"; members incl. The Edinburgh Bookshop, Nice Stuff, Paper Tiger. Warm aggregator. morningside-traders.co.uk.
  • Kevin Buckle — Avalanche Records owner + Edinburgh Evening News columnist on indie retail. Amplifier — but a sharp critic of hollow initiatives; win with substance (free-forever, never-charges-to-rank), not gloss. Has public friction with Roddy Smith.
  • Roddy Smith — CEO, Essential Edinburgh BID. Powerful, high credibility if won — but city-centre/paid-levy focused. ⚠ Handle neutrally; frame SMD as complementary to BIDs, never a rival.
  • Adjacent/precedent: Tom Harries — founder of "Edinburgh Lockdown Economy" free indie-listings site. Like-minded contact, not a champion.

  • Midlothian View (midlothianview.com) — indie news site with a business focus; already runs DMB stories. Best Midlothian coverage channel.

West Lothian

  • West Lothian Courier — coverage via newsdesk (no named business reporter surfaced).
  • Gap: no verified active traders' spokesperson in Bathgate/Whitburn.

National credibility (warm → aspirational)

  • Colin Borland — FSB Scotland Director (warm, easy). Public voice on high streets; FSB already campaigns on Lothian issues (opposed North Berwick parking charges). Good for a supportive quote + reaching member shops. @colin_borland / @FSB_Scotland. (Chair: Guy Hinks.)
  • Dr Pete Cheema OBE — CEO, Scottish Grocers' Federation (warm, on-message): "People in Scotland have embraced local… we cannot take it for granted that they will stay there." Access to independent convenience retailers.
  • Neil McInroy — Community Wealth Building global lead (aspirational): architect of Scotland's CWB agenda — philosophically identical to SMD. Good for a thought-leadership/credibility quote.
  • David Lonsdale — Scottish Retail Consortium (aspirational, big-retail-leaning): authority/credibility, less grassroots.
  • Media beyond local: no single national indie-retail columnist "owns" this beat — pitch the story to Scottish Grocer, The Sentinel, Edinburgh Reporter instead of courting a named writer.

Handling rules (important)

  • Politicians = strictly cross-party. Approach Johnson (Lab) and Hyslop (SNP) with identical neutral framing; seek comment on the mission, not the party. Same for East Lothian councillors (Craig Hoy MSP ran a "Support Our High Streets" campaign but is a party politician — invite all members equally).
  • North Berwick / Susan Oliver — positive angle only; do not touch the live parking dispute.
  • BIDs (Roddy Smith etc.) — always "complements your BID," never "replaces." "Free, never charges to rank" is the differentiator vs paid directories — lead with it.
  • Kevin Buckle — substance over spin, or he'll say so in print.
  • Scotland's Towns Partnership — address Guthrie, not Prentice; position as delivering Love Local locally.
