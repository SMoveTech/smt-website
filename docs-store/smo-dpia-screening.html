S-Move Operations — DPIA Screening Assessment

A short screening to decide whether a full Data Protection Impact Assessment is legally required for the SMO platform, and to record the outcome. Companion to the Data Protection / GDPR doc.

Prepared 2026-07-29 on the interim safe basis. Controller: S-Move Removals Ltd (SMR), SC892140.

1. What this is

Under UK GDPR a full DPIA is mandatory only where processing is likely to result in a high risk to individuals. This screening tests SMO's processing against the ICO's high-risk criteria to decide whether a full DPIA is needed. It is deliberately proportionate to SMR's small scale.

2. Processing under assessment

  • Customer identity, addresses, move details and communications.
  • Staff HR data including bank details and payroll.
  • Financial/banking data (Monzo transaction import into the ledger).
  • AI processing of enquiries, receipts and quote context (Anthropic Claude).
  • Voice-call and SMS intake (Twilio); room photos uploaded for quoting.
  • Passkey (biometric-backed) authentication.

3. Screening against the ICO high-risk criteria

CriterionApplies?Notes
Evaluation or scoring / profilingNoNo profiling of individuals. AI structures enquiry data; it does not score or profile people.
Automated decision-making with legal / significant effectNoQuote pricing is decided by a human admin (the AI only suggests); no automated decision produces a legal/significant effect on anyone.
Systematic monitoring of a publicly accessible areaNoNo CCTV or public-space monitoring.
Special-category or criminal-offence dataNoNo health, biometric-as-identifier at scale, or criminal data is deliberately processed. Passkeys store a public-key credential, not a biometric template (biometrics stay on the user's device).
Data processed on a large scaleNoSmall removals business; low volume of data subjects. Not "large scale" in the ICO sense.
Matching or combining datasetsNoData is combined only within the single customer/job record for the service itself.
Data about vulnerable data subjectsNo (not targeted)General adult customers and employees; no targeting of children or vulnerable groups.
Innovative use of technologyPartlyAI (Claude) for enquiry handling and voice intake is innovative. Risk is limited: no special-category data, human stays in the loop, inputs aren't used to train the model.
Prevents data subjects exercising a right or using a serviceNoProcessing enables the service; it does not block access or rights.
Biometric / genetic dataNoNo genetic data. Passkey biometrics never leave the user's device.
Tracking location or behaviourNoAddresses are collected to perform the move, not to track people.

4. Outcome

A full DPIA is not mandatory. The processing meets at most one of the ICO's high-risk indicators (innovative technology), and that risk is low and mitigated. Two indicators normally point toward a mandatory DPIA; SMO does not reach that threshold.

Residual risk is low-to-moderate, driven mainly by holding staff bank details and financial data. These are mitigated by the controls in the Data Protection doc §6: server-side-only database access, hashed passwords, restricted bank-details access, device-gated admin login, HTTPS-only, and hourly backups.

5. Re-screen if any of these change

  • Volume grows to a materially larger scale.
  • Special-category data starts being processed (e.g. health information about a customer's needs).
  • Automated pricing or any automated decision with a significant effect goes live.
  • A new AI capability begins profiling or scoring individuals.
