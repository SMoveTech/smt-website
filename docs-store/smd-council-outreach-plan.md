# SMD — Council Outreach Plan (Edinburgh, East Lothian, Midlothian, West Lothian)

*Compiled 27 July 2026. All contacts are from publicly available sources. Council roles change — confirm names on the council site before addressing correspondence to a named person. Where no individual is publicly listed, a team inbox is given (never invent a name).*

**What we're asking councils for:** (a) introductions to independent shops / inclusion in their business comms, (b) co-promotion through their "shop local" channels, (c) town-centre / BID partnership and a possible pilot town, (d) a conversation about relevant funding.

**The pitch, in one line:** a free-forever, community-first directory + marketplace that turns "shop local" sentiment into actual local sales, never charges shops to exist or to rank — i.e. the discovery/transaction layer their existing directories and campaigns don't have.

---

## The universal playbook (applies to all four)

1. **Two front doors per council, hit in parallel:**
   - **Economic Development team** (owns the strategy, the funding, and the relationships with BIDs/traders) → the "official" route.
   - **The BID / town-centre body** (member org of the exact indie shops we want) → the *fast* route to warm shop introductions. A BID mailout reaches shops quicker than a council can.
2. **Frame to their language:** every one of these councils uses **"Community Wealth Building"** and/or **town-centre vitality / inclusive growth**. Lead with keeping spend local + banding independents together. It maps to SMD almost word-for-word.
3. **Kill the "who profits / is this a cost?" objection early:** free to list, never pay-to-rank, mission-driven. Councils can promote it without budget or procurement.
4. **Complement, don't compete:** East Lothian and Midlothian already run *free directories*. Explicitly position SMD as the discover-and-buy layer on top, not a replacement.
5. **Local credibility:** home-grown East Lothian initiative (Ormiston, EH35 5LE). Leads with East Lothian; useful everywhere.
6. **Officer first, councillor second:** open with the officer/team; bring in the portfolio councillor for political backing once there's a live conversation.

**Recommended sequence:** ① East Lothian (home turf, warmest) → prove the model → ② use it as a reference when approaching Midlothian & West Lothian (both have obvious pilot towns/BIDs) → ③ Edinburgh last (biggest, most complex, best reached via its BIDs).
