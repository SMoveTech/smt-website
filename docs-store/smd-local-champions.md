# SMD — Local Champions & Allies (people who could publicly support us)

*Compiled 27 July 2026. Every name is from a public source. Roles change and some are mid-dispute or political — see handling notes. Never invent a quote; confirm current role before approaching by name.*

**Goal:** find people already campaigning for local/independent high streets who'd lend a voice, an intro to shops, or coverage. Ranked warmest-first. SMD = free community directory + marketplace, free for shops, never charges to rank, high-street-revival mission.

---

## ⭐ Top 6 to approach first (best warmth × leverage)

1. **Scotland's Towns Partnership — Kimberley Guthrie (Chief Officer)** *(national, warmest partner)*
   Runs **Scotland Loves Local** — the national shop-local campaign + gift card (physical-premises only, so SMD's online directory *complements* it). Comes with a ready-made distribution channel (Love Local Week, partner editorial). **Frame as "we deliver Love Local on the ground in the Lothians," never a rival.** Route: lovelocal.scot/contact-us · @scotlandstowns · 45 King Street, Stirling. *(Address to Guthrie — NOT former chief Phil Prentice, who stepped back.)*

2. **Philip Mellor — Chair, Dunbar Trades' Association** *(East Lothian, mission-identical)*
   DTA already runs offline "Shop Local – Win Local" + "Spot the Malarky" campaigns with Visit East Lothian. SMD is the online version of what he already does. Route: dunbar.org.uk/dta · facebook.com/DunbarTradesAssociation.

3. **Emma Bouglet — Business Manager, East Lothian Food & Drink (BID)** *(East Lothian, multiplier)*
   Scotland's first food-&-drink BID; her public words are textbook SMD ("every purchase with an independent… strengthens the fabric of our communities"). Reaches many member businesses at once. Route: scotlandsfooddrinkcounty.com.

4. **Mark Darragh — Deputy Chair, One Linlithgow BID** *(West Lothian, near-verbatim pitch match)*
   Owner of Play Bugs; launched Scotland Loves Local Week for Linlithgow — "Every time someone chooses local, they're making an investment in our community." Warmest single indie voice found. Route: onelinlithgow.com. (Operational partner: **Tony I'Anson**, BID Manager.)

5. **Tom Donaldson — Chair, Dalkeith Means Business** *(Midlothian, pilot partner)*
   Business-led group *actively building a town directory* + testing a BID, backed by Scotland's Towns Partnership. SMD directly serves what he's trying to build. Route: dalkeithmeansbusiness.co.uk. (Comms/route-in: **Jill Simon**.)

6. **Daniel Johnson MSP (Lab, Edinburgh Southern)** *(political — the standout)*
   A **Lothian MSP who was himself an independent retailer** (ex-MD of Paper Tiger/Studio One, Morningside Traders member); convened the Economy & Fair Work Committee's town-centres inquiry. The most credible political voice for SMD. **Keep cross-party** — pair with **Fiona Hyslop MSP (SNP, Linlithgow)** for West Lothian so no endorsement reads as partisan. Route: constituency office / parliament.scot.

- **Kevin Buckle** — substance over spin, or he'll say so in print.
- **Scotland's Towns Partnership** — address Guthrie, not Prentice; position as delivering Love Local locally.
