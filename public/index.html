What we build

Two platforms, one engineering standard.

Each product removes a genuine day-to-day headache for the businesses that use it — with the reliability, clarity and honesty we'd want from software ourselves.

Operations Platform

S-Move Operations

An end-to-end operating system for removals companies — quoting, booking, scheduling, customer portal, invoicing and payments in one place, replacing the usual tangle of spreadsheets and messages.

  • Instant quotes and online booking
  • Customer portal with live job tracking
  • Invoicing, payments & financial reporting
  • Also available to other removals companies
Live & in production
Marketplace Platform

S-Move Dealer

A discovery platform that helps independent shops and dealers put their one-off and second-hand stock online — giving distinctive local businesses a fair shopfront on the internet.

  • Free-forever core listing tools
  • Ranking earned by genuine interest, never bought
  • Optional supporter tier for local businesses
  • Built on an honest, charitable foundation
In beta — launching soon

The company

S-Move Technologies Ltd

S-Move Technologies is a private limited software company based near Edinburgh, Scotland. We design, build and operate the platforms behind the S-Move name — creating well-made software for industries that have long been underserved by it.

We are independently owned and self-directed, which lets us build for the long term and put the interests of the businesses and people who use our software ahead of everything else.
