Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

This policy explains how S-Move Technologies Ltd ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this website, smt.s-move.co.uk. We are the data controller for the purposes of UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect

This is a simple informational website. We do not require you to create an account or submit forms to use it. We may process:

  • Contact information you send us — if you email or call us using the details on this site, we hold that correspondence in order to reply and keep a record of our dealings with you.
  • Basic technical data — our hosting provider automatically logs standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify individuals.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on this website, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

Why we process it & our legal basis

We process contact data to respond to enquiries and manage our business relationships (our legitimate interests, and where relevant, steps taken at your request before entering a contract). We process technical logs for the legitimate interest of keeping the site secure and available.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, then delete it. Server logs are retained only for a short period.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below.

Our products

The platforms we operate (such as S-Move Operations and S-Move Dealer) have their own privacy terms presented within those products. This policy covers only this marketing website.
